Premise
A Zoom plugin that leverages and enhances the collaboration tool by creating 1-click group games that are easy enough for your mother to facilitate.  
AI: NOW facilitated by an AI host that you can scale up or down the humor.  Turn on or off adult humor.  Turn on or off insults.  Pick your humor based on a comedian to host?
